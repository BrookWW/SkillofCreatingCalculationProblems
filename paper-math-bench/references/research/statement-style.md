## Compact calculation targets

State one coherent requested calculation. Introduce only the objects, conventions, parameter ranges, and assumptions needed to determine that target. Prefer familiar mathematical language and compact formulas when they make the task easier to read. Keep the statement self-contained without explaining standard terminology solely because a longer definition is possible.

Use a short answer contract that fixes the required form and accuracy. Leave decisive representations, structural choices, and intermediate discoveries to the solver. Omit source hints, method names that reveal the route, unused background, redundant symbols, and leading subparts. When definitions overwhelm the target, reconsider the formulation or mathematical object rather than compressing away necessary information.

## Necessary mathematical structure

Make the principal source's connected mechanisms consequential to the requested value. A useful discovery resolves a non-obvious obstruction and determines a later choice or part of the answer. Routine algebra, extra notation, and disconnected techniques do not create depth.

Related literature can expose both reusable mechanisms and the boundary of their applicability. Verify any bridge across that boundary and make its mathematical effect necessary to the target. A published solution route can guide construction, but it does not prove that a shorter route is impossible. Continue to challenge the strongest credible answer route under the [construction and adversarial review policy](problem-refinement.md#construction-and-adversarial-review).

## Reference completeness and review

A concise statement can require a substantial complete reference. Verify all requested cases, constants, structural choices, and explicit proof obligations before model diagnosis or screening. Distinguish the work needed to calculate the answer from additional work proving validity; a difficult validity proof does not increase the difficulty of an answer available by an easier route.

Use these considerations through [References in design](problem-design.md#references-in-design), with depth chosen for the actual task. They prescribe neither a construction template nor a discovery count. The [independent statement review](statement-review.md) remains mandatory immediately before final formal screening, and test-based groups follow the [formal-test policy](../evaluation/model-testing.md#screen-authoring-candidates).
