## Scope and inputs

Find selects literature with credible potential for hard calculation benchmarks. It can stand alone or precede Author when the user requests both discovery and construction. Accept paper URLs, arXiv identifiers and local documents.

The discovery platform is arXiv. Before new principal-paper discovery, follow [startup intake](../execution/task-settings.md#startup-intake) to resolve the user's date range, principal-paper count, and subject scope, including inside continuous-duration and accepted-question-count authoring goals. Offer the last 10 natural days including today as a date choice, or a user-specified interval; the default still needs an actual reply. Resolve subjects through the multi-select or free-text intake, without an implicit analysis-only restriction. The date interval, paper count, and selected subject scope bound the source pool; the separate [work goal](../execution/continuous-goals.md#scope-and-intake) determines duration or accepted-question count. Neither goal mode waives source intake. Use [paths and write scope](../execution/task-settings.md#paths-and-write-scope) for the working cache. Missing requirements and unavailable material follow [required materials and interruptions](../execution/task-settings.md#required-materials-and-interruptions). Find-only produces a candidate-source report, not a finished bank or model trials.

Use the absolute inclusive start/end dates and v1/first-submission UTC semantics in [startup intake](../execution/task-settings.md#startup-intake). Record those dates, the original anchor for an accepted relative interval, the principal-paper count and selected pool, the executed query, date field and UTC bounds, selected subjects and any free-text mapping to categories or search terms, retrieved versions and coverage limitations. Deduplicate versions and cross-listings. Keep bounded search results distinct from supplementary reading; background citations do not fill the requested pool or authorize expanding it. Supplementary reading may support verification, but promoting a reference to a principal source or replacing a principal paper must respect the agreed date/count bounds. Resolve a required scope change before that promotion or replacement. Identify the roles of the entry papers and actual principal sources.

## Inspect the principal mathematics

Download each paper selected for Find's final recommendations into the task cache and read its original full text from beginning to end, including appendices, before completing its opportunity assessment. An existing readable full-text copy in the cache can be reused. Search snippets, abstracts, summaries, or selected theorem extracts do not satisfy this requirement. It applies to selected principal papers, including a replacement selected through source discovery inside an authoring goal; it does not require reading every search hit. If a full text is unavailable through authorized access, do not claim its assessment is complete. The AI may select another source within scope; if that particular paper is required, follow up under [required materials](../execution/task-settings.md#required-materials-and-interruptions).

The AI chooses which search leads to pursue and which reviews, related papers, foundations, extensions, or alternative methods to read, in what order, and to what depth under [source research](problem-design.md#intake-and-sources). Revisit the [governing principles](../../SKILL.md) before each research judgment. Base the assessment on the mathematics actually established, record the full-text location and actual exposure, and distinguish checked mechanisms and hypotheses from remaining uncertainty.

Prefer papers whose main theorem itself resolves a substantial calculation, such as an extremal quantity, exact invariant, spectral problem, infinite series, counting function or asymptotic coefficient. Next consider calculations developed within a proof, when their mathematics can support a difficult observable. Merely changing an existence or proof question into a numerical label does not supply a calculation core.

## Assess calculation potential

Compare opportunities using the [target-selection and shortest-route standards](problem-design.md#choose-and-formulate). Keep a concise record that also serves as the handoff:

| Item | Mathematical evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Main theorem and source | Exact version and location, the computational object, and whether it is a main result or a calculation within a proof. |
| Proposed observable | A plausible self-contained quantity and answer contract; how the source mechanisms determine its value. |
| Necessary discoveries and shortest route | The consequential representation or structural choices, their dependencies, and the strongest credible direct, theorem-recognition or numerical route. |
| Feasibility | Checked hypotheses, a decisive bridge calculation or parameter test, the route to certifying an answer, and unresolved obligations. |

Evaluate the discovery of a correct representation separately from routine execution after it is known. A long expansion, many parameters or high precision does not replace necessary discoveries; conversely, standard tools in a completed calculation do not make its representation obvious.

Carry out the mathematical check that decides whether a proposed opportunity is feasible. A claimed cross-literature connection follows [cross-literature synthesis](problem-refinement.md#cross-literature-synthesis). A numerical stationary point or an unchecked theorem application remains a lead with a stated gap. Find does not require the completed reference demanded by Author, but a reading list or an unsupported claim of depth is not a completed opportunity assessment.

## Report and handoff

Prioritize the assessed opportunities with concrete reasons: ready for Author, promising with a named unresolved check, or not preferred because of a demonstrated reduction or mismatch. Difficulty is provisional; a source recommendation is not benchmark admission.

The requested paper count is not a problem count. Return fewer papers when the bounded search does not support enough recommendations, explaining the coverage and reasons rather than filling the list. Report checked opportunities or a supported no-fit result within the agreed scope.

For Find-only, deliver the report in the working cache. For a request that already includes construction, hand the source roles, mathematical record and remaining checks to [Author](problem-design.md); its elaboration, complete-reference and formal-screening contract remains unchanged.
