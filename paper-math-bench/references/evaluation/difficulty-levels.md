## Difficulty principles

Assess background, conceptual discovery, and execution separately. Judge the strongest credible route to the requested output. A conceptual leap finds a non-obvious representation, structure, construction, or consequential choice that determines the answer. Technical vocabulary, concealed definitions, routine steps, and leap counts do not measure it.

Default to the strongest verified candidate attainable under this skill's highest mathematical standards and agreed resources; respect explicit user overrides without asking for a startup tier selection. Build depth into target selection and prefer consequential, connected discoveries while keeping the statement compact. Apply the [authoring criteria](../research/problem-design.md#choose-and-formulate) to the mathematical gate and [elaboration](../research/problem-refinement.md) to long-chain target selection, cross-literature synthesis, adversarial construction, and continuation decisions.

Short solutions can be deep; long proofs can be routine. Paper length, formula complexity, parameter count, and a single model failure establish neither an official human difficulty level nor originality. The final local T2/T3/T4 groups instead follow [formal test outcomes](model-testing.md#screen-authoring-candidates). For calculation requests, a difficult validity proof cannot promote an answer obtainable by an easier standard route. Refer to the selected model and budget, solve trajectories and accounting, statement-style guidance, and literature under [References in design](../research/problem-design.md#references-in-design); decide their use and weight from the mathematical problem and explain their effect on the assessment. A human-difficulty estimate needs a defensible account of the strongest credible answer route; it is separate from the operational test-based group and cannot override it.

## Tier reference

| Reference tier | Human difficulty anchor |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Strong undergraduate or Olympiad-level insight, with advanced tools or coding available. |
| 2 | Advanced graduate work; an expert may need a day for the idea and calculation. |
| 3 | Early research-level work; specialized insight and several days of expert effort. |
| 4 | A substantial short research project, developed by experienced researchers over weeks. |

## Local authoring gate

Apply [the mathematical gate](../research/problem-design.md#choose-and-formulate) to well-posedness, necessary discoveries, source connections, the strongest answer route, and reference completeness. Use the Tier 4 human anchor as the default construction ambition, not as a required forecast. Repair validity and reference defects before a trial; use depth judgments to improve or redirect construction. Every selected final question receives a formal test and the [local test-based group](model-testing.md#screen-authoring-candidates). The AI decides final selection under [post-trial research judgment](../research/problem-refinement.md#post-trial-research-decisions); it cannot change a recorded T4 classification merely because a reviewer suspects an easier route. These are local authoring and grouping rules, not Epoch tier certification.

## Applying the guide

Explain the decisive discoveries, their necessary dependencies, and the strongest solution route in a concise private assessment. Use the [statement-style guidance](../research/statement-style.md) and the tier descriptions here at the depth useful for the task. Their use follows [References in design](../research/problem-design.md#references-in-design), rather than a prescribed construction or discovery count. Interpret intermediate simulations under [anonymous diagnostics](../research/problem-design.md#anonymous-diagnostics), separately from formal target-model screening; neither supplies official tier certification.
