## Scope and intake

Full authoring and sustained elaboration use exactly two work-goal modes: continuous work for a user-specified duration, or continued research until a user-specified number of selected local T4 questions is complete. Find, Author, Elaborator, and Evaluation are task entry points, not additional goal modes. Resolve source/task scope first, then the separate goal mode and value through [intake](task-settings.md#startup-intake). An explicit duration or accepted-question count already identifies its goal mode; a paper count does not. If neither goal is supplied for sustained work, ask which mode and value to use. If both are supplied and their stopping relationship is unclear, ask rather than choosing one silently. Reuse all other established parameters. A bounded, explicitly untested preview or bounded standalone refinement does not require an additional H/K goal questionnaire; an explicit continuous duration still governs such a task.

User-specified duration, question count, source scope, model configuration, and write scope are binding. The AI chooses intermediate research actions, source allocation, revisions, abandonment, and final selection under [elaboration](../research/problem-refinement.md#post-trial-research-decisions). Do not add fixed revision counts, similarity scores, mandatory review rounds, per-paper production quotas, or other mechanical research thresholds. The usual 0–3-per-paper batch scale is guidance; explicit user goals and source limits govern the work. Mathematical correctness and truthful reporting remain required. These restrictions on research quotas do not waive the [mandatory statement review before final formal testing](../research/statement-review.md).

Source discovery uses arXiv and requires a bounded source scope under [startup intake](task-settings.md#startup-intake): start date, end date, and principal-paper count. Both goal modes require these fields when searching for new papers. A six-hour goal and a goal of completing several questions govern work; neither replaces the paper-date interval or the number of source papers. Research choices and source allocation remain autonomous within the established pool. Paper replacement or supplementary reading must not bypass the pool's date/count bounds; ask about a necessary scope change instead of silently expanding it. Reuse resolved scope fields rather than restarting a separate Find questionnaire.

For full authoring and elaboration that includes formal screening, both modes target selected local T4 questions unless the user explicitly requests broader final output. A duration alone does not authorize target-model trials in standalone elaboration: without testing in scope, retain its explicitly unscreened working-version output. A T4-count goal includes the screening needed to establish that count. Lower-tier or otherwise unselected verified versions remain useful working evidence rather than automatically entering the final bank. A local T4 group follows the existing [formal-test policy](../evaluation/model-testing.md#screen-authoring-candidates), not a human-difficulty forecast. Explicitly untested previews retain their authorized output contract and cannot satisfy a T4 count; clarify an incompatible request for both an untested preview and a completed T4 count. Find-only and standalone evaluation retain their existing scope and do not require these goal parameters.

## Continuous duration

Use the positive duration supplied by the user, such as six continuous hours. Start measuring when required intake, including the applicable paper-date interval and principal-paper count, is resolved and execution begins. Do not begin the research window while awaiting source scope. Track elapsed wall time in the current continuous task, including research, verification, and model trials; do not substitute a scheduled start, periodic wakeup, sum of worker times, or count of messages. Preserve the start and remaining duration across context compaction or internal handoffs, rather than restarting the clock. Apply the [six-minute reply allowance](task-settings.md#required-materials-and-interruptions) to missing input during work, without silently changing the fixed goal or deadline, treating silence as consent, or reporting blocked waiting as productive continuous work.

Continue the research loop during this window. A first tested question, a solved version, or the end of one paper does not itself end the duration goal. The AI decides how to spend the remaining time, including changing core or paper within scope. If essential input or execution limits prevent continued work, follow up in the conversation and report the actual interruption; do not claim uninterrupted work that did not occur.

At the duration boundary, stop initiating research and trials. Use available process cancellation to end in-flight work when possible, preserving its true execution state. A trial interrupted by the overall duration is pending, not a mathematical failure or token-budget exhaustion. Calls that cannot be cancelled may finish later; record their actual timing and do not pretend they were completed before the boundary. Finish the authorized delivery checks and report any additional wrap-up time separately. Do not silently extend the research window.

## T4 question count

Use the positive integer count supplied by the user. Continue researching, reconstructing, discarding candidates, or replacing papers until that many distinct selected questions meet the existing mathematical and exact-version formal-test requirements and are classified local T4. The AI judges whether they are substantively different mathematical questions. They may share a mathematical core; parameter substitutions, equivalent rephrasings, multiple versions, or repeated trials of one question do not by themselves increase the count. Resolve that judgment from the mathematics without imposing core-diversity quotas or a numeric similarity threshold.

Count only completed selected questions with their verified references and bound formal-test evidence. Candidate files, unfinished stronger versions, pending reviews, execution errors, interrupted trials, and unscreened previews do not count. Do not change the test policy, relabel lower tiers, or repeat an unchanged version until it happens to fail. Revisions with changed mathematical content receive their own reference and trial before they can count, while the same final question is counted once. This selected-question count is separate from evaluation reporting, which retains every assigned task and its original denominator.

Do not invent an overall time, paper, or round limit when the user has not specified one. Preserve actual platform and resource limits. A count goal does not guarantee that enough qualifying mathematics exists in the permitted source scope. If the AI cannot make credible progress, report the current count and mathematical situation and ask about a necessary scope or goal change rather than declaring success or manufacturing busywork. Ordinary difficulty or one rejected direction is not itself an external blocker.

## Goal execution and progress

When the user authorizes execution in either goal mode and required intake is complete, use the available Goal mechanism for the current task. Record the fixed mode and value together with the separate source scope (supplied paper pool, or arXiv dates and paper count) in its objective. Do not replace a paper-count boundary with a work-duration goal or a question-count goal. Inspect and reuse a suitable unfinished goal rather than silently replacing it. Planning a workflow or editing this skill does not itself authorize starting a mathematical research goal. Do not create a scheduled automation, heartbeat, recurring job, or separate chat as a substitute for continuous work.

Use Goal tools according to their runtime contract. Their objective and optional token-budget fields do not provide native duration or question-count enforcement. Track the chosen goal in the task cache and check it when choosing the next action and around long-running operations. Set a Goal token budget only if the user explicitly requests it; the default budget for an individual screening attempt is not a budget for the entire authoring goal. If the Goal mechanism is unavailable, report the limitation, ask a focused question when needed, and continue any independent authorized work; do not claim activation or substitute scheduled execution.

Keep a compact current record of the mode and requested value, start and elapsed time or accepted T4 count, active mathematical direction, selected question identities, and version/trial bindings. Explain material decisions briefly using actual mathematical findings. These records support continuation and truthful counting; they do not prescribe a new research bureaucracy or belong in the final manuscript. Report interruptions, remaining work, and independently unknown runtime metadata accurately.

## Completion and delivery

The duration goal is achieved after the requested continuous work has actually been carried out and its results honestly delivered; it does not guarantee a nonzero T4 yield. If unresolved required input prevents fulfilling the continuous-work commitment, report the interruption and unmet goal rather than declaring completion merely because the deadline passed; do not automatically extend the fixed deadline. The count goal is complete only when the requested number of distinct selected local T4 questions and the authorized final-bank checks are finished. Fewer accepted questions, exhausted resources, missing input, or an abandoned paper do not satisfy the count objective. Respect explicit user stop or pause requests and the Goal tool's status rules.

Deliver only the selected versions that meet the agreed output target, with the matching answers, complete references, sources, test classifications, and version bindings. Preserve all trial evidence in the cache, including lower-tier, unsuccessful, and unselected versions. Apply the existing [append/replacement scope](task-settings.md#paths-and-write-scope) and [manuscript checks](../research/problem-design.md#present-and-write); a new work goal does not authorize deleting or rewriting older bank entries. Report achieved and requested results separately, including zero accepted T4 questions when that is the outcome.
